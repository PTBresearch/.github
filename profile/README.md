## Welcome to the Open Source Repository of PTB

🙋‍♀️ We are **PTBresearch**, the GitHub repository of the [Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt](https://www.ptb.de/), the German National Metrology Institute .

📍 We are located in Brunswick and Berlin in Germany. We are proud to say that the scope of our expertise and passion encompasses all conceivable measurement units (in accordance with the SI system), with the objective of achieving the utmost precision and accuracy in our measurements.

🌈 We are committed to Open Knowledge and Open Source. We value and support this by relasing our research findings via [Open Access](https://oar.ptb.de/) and our software via [Open Source](https://gitlab1.ptb.de/explore).

👩‍💻 More software projects can be found in the repositories below. Enjoy.🍿
