## Welcome to the Open Source Repository of PTB

🙋‍♀️ We are **PTBresearch**, the GitHub repository of the [Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt](https://www.ptb.de/), the German National Metrology Institute .

📍 We are located in Brunswick and Berlin in Germany. We are proud to say that the scope of our expertise and passion encompasses all conceivable measurement units (in accordance with the [SI system](https://si-digital-framework.org/)). Our objective is to achieve the utmost precision and accuracy in our measurements.

🌈 We are committed to Open Knowledge and Open Source. We value and support this by releasing our research findings via [Open Access](https://oar.ptb.de/) and our software via [Open Source](https://gitlab1.ptb.de/explore).

🧑‍🎓 If you want to learn more about us, have a look at [digital.ptb.de](https://digital.ptb.de). If you are interested in joining us (as a researcher, PhD, student, intern, ...), have a look at our [career site](https://www.ptb.de/cms/en/about-us-careers/careers/bms-stellen.html).

👩‍💻 More software projects can be found in the repositories below. Enjoy.🍿
